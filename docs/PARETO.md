# Roster Pareto Analysis

How the candidate pool spreads across **size** (parameters) and **capability** (the 5 `qualityBy` axes), and the
**frontier-tolerance gate** that turns it into the active roster.

The pool is every open-weight checkpoint with an architecture record (`data/model-arch.json`): **135 candidates**
(52 hand-authored in `src/sim/content.ts` + 83 generated by `scripts/gen-roster.mjs`). A
**6% frontier-tolerance gate** (`withinFrontierTolerance`, `src/sim/calibrate.ts`) keeps **58 active**
checkpoints — the ones the player owns and deploys.

Every number below is computed from the *actual loaded roster* (`MODEL_CANDIDATES` / `MODEL_DEFS`, calibrated by
`src/sim/calibrate.ts`) by `node scripts/roster-report.mjs pareto`. A **frontier** is the (minimize size, maximize
quality) upper envelope: at each size, the best quality attainable on that axis by a model no larger.

| 400B–1T | 20 | 8 | 105 | 108 | 119 | 127 | 129 |
| >1T | 5 | 4 | 106 | 108 | 121 | 128 | 130 |

**Capability compression:** the best 15–40B checkpoint matches or beats the whole 40–120B band on every axis
(chat 100 vs 97, general 101 vs 95, coding 105 vs 90, reasoning 117 vs 104, agentic 125 vs 92) — stacking parameters in that band buys
nothing, and the ceiling moves only with the multi-hundred-billion MoEs. The gate below keeps only the
checkpoints that are still a rational pick at their size.

## 3. The active roster — the 6% frontier-tolerance gate

A checkpoint stays iff, **on at least one capability axis, its quality is within 6% of the frontier at
its size** (`ROSTER_FRONTIER_TOLERANCE = 0.06`). A model is dropped only when it trails the frontier by more than
6% on every axis — strictly beaten by something no bigger, on all five lanes. The gate trims **135
candidates → 58 active**, spanning 0.6B → 2.78T. The default starter
(`DEFAULT_MODEL_ID`) is the smallest dense, non-thinking active model: **Qwen3.5 0.8B**.
